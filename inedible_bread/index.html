01 — THE EVIDENCE

Three reasons bread has failed humanity.

The case against bread is not emotional. It is scientific. Or at least, it sounds scientific if you don't think about it for too long.

ARGUMENT 001

Texture Crisis

Bread is simultaneously too soft and too hard. The crust resists your teeth while the interior collapses into a suspicious cloud of crumbs. It cannot commit to a texture.

ARGUMENT 002

Structural Failure

Apply the slightest pressure and bread disintegrates. A food that cannot survive being held has failed the most basic engineering test imaginable.

ARGUMENT 003

Existential Deception

Bread advertises itself as a meal, yet often requires additional ingredients to become interesting. Is bread food, or merely a delivery mechanism for better food?

"If bread were truly edible, why does it need butter, cheese, or an entire sandwich to justify its existence?"
02 — THE COUNTERARGUMENT

"But bread tastes good."

A common defense. A weak one. Taste is subjective. The structural integrity of a bread-based object collapsing into crumbs is not. We must separate emotional attachment from objective truth.

03 — FINAL VERDICT

The truth is
in the crumbs.

Bread has had thousands of years to prove itself. And yet, here we are, still pretending that baked flour is a meaningful contribution to civilization.
